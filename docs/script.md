# The Fallen Star / 坠落的星星 — Script (draft 1)

Subtitles only (EN / 中文). Lines are short. Times are inside each scene.
Art: illustrators' package (B1–B9, C1–C7, P1, T1). Code in src2/sNN.js, each with a fixed DURATION.

## S01 · Morning on B612 — 300 s (B1 → B2)
- 0–20: Paper fades to B1. Slow push in. Birds cross. Pip's future place in the sky is not visible yet (day).
- 20–70: The boy (C6 walking) waters the rose. The sheep (C5) trots behind, nose in the watering can. Gag: the sheep sneezes from the rose's pollen; the rose huffs.
  - ROSE: "Careful. I am very delicate." / 小心点，我很娇贵的。
- 70–130: Baobab sprouts. The sheep nibbles one, pulls, falls on its bum. Pulls a second, a third. The boy claps.
  - BOY: "Good sheep! Three before breakfast." / 好羊！早饭前就拔了三棵。
- 130–180: Sweeping the volcanoes. The sheep's tail used as a duster. Soot face. Both laugh (laughing heads).
- 180–230: Dissolve B1 → B2 (dusk). They sit on the hill edge. The first stars come out one by one.
- 230–280: The boy points at one star, a little brighter than the rest, and it twinkles.
  - BOY: "That one belongs to a friend of mine." / 那颗星星属于我的一个朋友。
  - BOY: "He lives on Earth. When he looks at it, he laughs." / 他住在地球上。他看着它，就会笑。
  - The star twinkles back, almost like a giggle.
- 280–300: **TITLE** T1 "The Fallen Star / 坠落的星星" over the dusk sky.

## S02 · The star falls — 300 s (B2 night grade)
- 0–40: Night. The boy and sheep asleep (C5/C6 sleeping). The friend's star hiccups — flicker, flicker.
- 40–80: It wobbles, slips, and streaks down across the sky. Whoosh. A soft *plink* in the rose's garden. The glass dome rings.
- 80–130: The sheep wakes, creeps over. In the grass: a tiny star (C1 pale, eyes closed), trembling.
- 130–200: First meeting. The sheep sniffs; Pip opens its eyes, squeaks (chime), hides behind the rose. The rose, surprisingly, shelters it.
  - ROSE: "Well? Don't just stand there. It's cold." / 愣着干嘛？它冷着呢。
- 200–260: The sheep lies down and curls around Pip like a warm wool nest. Pip glows a little brighter. Cosy.
- 260–300: The boy wakes, sees them, looks up: a dark gap in the sky where the friend's star was.
  - BOY: "Oh no. That's his star." / 糟了。那是他的星星。

## S03 · Grey by tomorrow — 300 s (B1 morning, B9 desert)
- 0–60: Morning. Pip is paler than last night. Each minute a little greyer (C1 pale → grey tint).
- 60–130: The boy fetches his little telescope. Iris shot → B9: the desert at night far away, the pilot by his plane (pilot overlay/rear view) looks up. Searches the sky. His shoulders drop.
  - PILOT (small, far): "...Where did you go?" / ……你去哪儿了？
- 130–190: Back on B612. The boy decides.
  - BOY: "We have to take it home. Before its light goes out." / 我们得送它回家。在它的光熄灭之前。
- 190–250: Problem: how to carry a star? The sheep drags out the old cardboard box from film 1. Pip hops in. The box glows through its three holes → **P1 box lantern**. 
- 250–300: The migratory birds arrive. Strings, the boy holds on, the sheep holds the lantern in its mouth. Goodbye to the rose (she pretends not to cry). Lift off.
  - ROSE: "Go. And come back." / 去吧。要回来。

## S04 · The Quiet — 300 s (B3)
- 0–60: Flying out into deep dark. No sound but wind. Stars far away.
- 60–120: Pip goes pale, then grey. The boy is worried and quiet. The sheep is scared too.
- 120–200: The sheep tries to cheer the boy: a silly upside-down dance on the birds' strings, ears flapping. The boy snorts, then laughs. Pip — a little chime, a giggle — glows gold for a moment.
- 200–260: They notice. Test it: the boy pulls a face; the sheep laughs; Pip glows.
  - BOY: "It's laughing that does it!" / 是笑声！笑声让它亮起来！
- 260–300: They fly on, laughing at each other's faces. A small gold trail behind them.

## S05 · The Clockmaker — 300 s (B4, C3)
- 0–40: Land on a planet full of clocks. Ticking everywhere. Sunset glow.
- 40–110: The tiny Clockmaker (C3 winding) rushes from clock to clock. No time to talk.
  - CLOCKMAKER: "No time, no time! If I stop, they stop!" / 没时间，没时间！我一停，它们就停了！
- 110–170: The sheep tries to help, gets wound up in a chain; slapstick. Its tail gets stuck in a big clock's gears — the clock stops. Silence.
- 170–240: Clockmaker frozen in horror… nothing bad happens. He has a free minute. He looks up — the sunset. First time in years.
  - CLOCKMAKER: "Oh. I forgot it was so pretty." / 哦。我都忘了，它这么美。
- 240–300: He laughs (C3 standing smile). Pip glows gold. He gives them a tiny gift: a pocket watch "for when you need a minute". Lift off.

## S06 · The Cloud — 300 s (B5 rain → B5 after rain, C4)
- 0–50: A planet in endless soft rain. Everyone dripping. Pip grey under the drizzle.
- 50–110: The Cloud (C4 sad raining) sniffles. She sees the sheep: woolly, white. She thinks it's a baby cloud.
  - CLOUD: "A little cloud! Are you lost too?" / 一朵小云！你也迷路了吗？
- 110–180: The sheep goes along with it — floats on the birds, puffs up its wool. They play tag around the planet. Rain stops in patches.
- 180–240: The Cloud laughs (C4 happy). Rain stops. Dissolve to B5 after rain. Rainbow.
- 240–300: Pip blazes bright gold. The Cloud waves goodbye; a small rainbow follows them for a while.

## S07 · Moth takes Pip — 300 s (B3 / B6 edge)
- 0–60: Night again. Calm flying. The boy dozes; the sheep keeps watch with the glowing lantern.
- 60–120: A shadow. Something huge and soft brushes past. Wing dust. The birds panic.
- 120–180: Moth (C2 open) swoops down and grabs the lantern. The sheep holds on, gets shaken off. The birds scatter.
- 180–240: The boy and sheep drift down, land on a small rock. The lantern light fades into the dark.
- 240–300: The sheep looks where it went: a dark planet with a faint violet glow (B6 on the horizon). It decides.

## S08 · The dark planet — 300 s (B6, C2)
- 0–60: The sheep walks alone across the dark planet. Dead trees. Very quiet.
- 60–120: Finds Moth curled up (folded wings), hugging the lantern like a hot-water bottle. Pip inside, grey.
- 120–180: Moth is not a monster. Shivering.
  - MOTH: "It's so cold here. I just wanted some light." / 这里太冷了。我只是想要一点光。
  - The sheep understands. It doesn't shout.
- 180–240: The sheep snuggles against Moth, wool on fur. It tickles. Moth giggles, then laughs (laughing face). Pip flickers gold. Moth's whole face lights up.
- 240–300: Moth gives the lantern back and asks to come along. The boy arrives, breathless; they all leave together, Moth carrying them.
  - MOTH: "Can I come? I'll carry you." / 我能一起去吗？我来驮你们。

## S09 · The meteor storm — 300 s (B7 clean + rocks)
- 0–60: Fast flight on Moth's back. Rocks appear, small, then big.
- 60–150: The storm. Moth dodges; rocks tumble past (B7 rock props). The sheep shields the lantern with its body.
- 150–200: A rock clips them. The lantern falls open. Pip goes completely grey and silent. Silence.
- 200–270: In the quiet, drifting, they start telling their happiest memories. The boy: the rose's first bloom. Moth: the first time he saw a light. The sheep: the boy saying "That's exactly the sheep I wanted." They laugh through tears.
- 270–300: A tiny flicker. Then a chime. Pip is alive.

## S10 · The star stairs — 300 s (B8)
- 0–60: Beyond the storm: a spiral staircase of stars rising into deep blue.
- 60–180: Moth carries them up, step by step. Each star they pass chimes hello. Pip chimes back.
- 180–240: At the top: one empty gap. They put Pip in its place. It fits.
- 240–300: But Pip is still faint. Small. Nobody down there has seen it yet. They wait, holding their breath.

## S11 · The pilot laughs — 270 s (B9 + pilot)
- 0–70: Earth, the desert, night. The pilot comes out of his tent, sits on the plane's wheel, looks up as every night.
- 70–140: He searches. Then — there. Faint, but there. He blinks. He smiles. He laughs out loud (C7 laughing).
  - PILOT: "There you are." / 你在这儿啊。
- 140–210: The laugh rises as a little gold ribbon of light, up through the night, all the way up the sky.
- 210–270: It reaches Pip. Pip blazes. And then all the stars start laughing like five hundred million little bells.

## S12 · Home — 250 s (B1 dawn grade, B2)
- 0–60: Moth flies them home in the dawn. B612 appears.
- 60–130: The rose, pretending she wasn't waiting.
  - ROSE: "You're late." / 你们回来晚了。
  - She smiles.
- 130–190: Moth stays as a night lamp; he hangs the empty lantern by the rose and curls up near the volcano, warm.
- 190–230: Evening. The boy, the sheep and Moth lie in the grass. Pip winks from the sky.
  - BOY: "Every time you laugh, a star gets brighter." / 每次你笑，就有一颗星星更亮一点。
- 230–250: The sheep falls asleep, smiling. **The End / 完.**
